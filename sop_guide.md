# ERP SOP Guide

## Purpose

This guide provides high-level guidance for following standard ERP procedures.

## Standard Procedure

1. Identify the business process.
2. Confirm the relevant ERP module.
3. Confirm the user's functional role.
4. Review the applicable approved procedure.
5. Follow the documented process steps.
6. Verify the expected outcome.
7. If the issue involves system access, permissions, configuration, or an unexpected system behavior, escalate to the appropriate ERP support team.

## Important Notes

- This guide provides functional guidance only.
- It does not provide live ERP access.
- It does not execute ERP transactions.
- It does not modify ERP configuration.
- Actual screen names, transaction codes, approval rules, and permissions may vary by ERP implementation.
- Users should follow their organization's approved SOPs when available.
