# ERP Process Documentation

## Purpose

This document provides general process guidance for common ERP functional activities.

## 1. Procure-to-Pay (P2P)

The Procure-to-Pay process covers purchasing goods or services and processing the related supplier invoice.

### General Process Flow

1. Identify the business requirement.
2. Create a purchase requisition.
3. Obtain required approval.
4. Create or process the purchase order.
5. Receive the goods or services.
6. Record the goods receipt.
7. Verify the supplier invoice.
8. Complete the applicable financial processing.

### Functional Guidance

Users should follow their organization's configured approval workflow and ERP procedures.

---

## 2. Order-to-Cash (O2C)

The Order-to-Cash process covers customer orders through delivery and payment.

### General Process Flow

1. Receive the customer requirement.
2. Create the sales order.
3. Validate required customer and order information.
4. Process delivery.
5. Confirm goods or services provided.
6. Create the customer invoice.
7. Process applicable payment and reconciliation activities.

---

## 3. Record-to-Report (R2R)

The Record-to-Report process covers recording financial transactions and preparing financial reporting.

### General Process Flow

1. Record applicable business transactions.
2. Review accounting information.
3. Perform required reconciliations.
4. Process period-end activities.
5. Review financial information.
6. Prepare applicable reports.

---

## 4. ERP Support Principles

- Follow the organization's approved ERP procedures.
- Confirm the relevant ERP module before providing guidance.
- Consider the user's role and permissions.
- Do not assume access to live ERP data.
- Escalate system-access or configuration issues to the appropriate support team.
